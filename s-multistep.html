Multistep Conversions

Mass, volume and particles can all be converted into each other by going through moles. With the mole ratio, a mass of one substance can be turned into a mass of another.

The Mole Triangle

Every route goes through moles.

Mole Triangle

Mass, volume and number of particles are three different ways to describe how much of a substance there is. None of them can be converted directly into another. Moles are the hub that connects all three, so every conversion in this unit passes through moles.

  • The “mole triangle” is a visual aid to help convert between different stoichiometric quantities.
  • Steps to Use the Mole Triangle:
    1. Start at the given unit on the mole triangle.
    2. Move in the direction of the desired unit.
    3. Follow the calculation shown on each arrow.

Two conversion factors: into moles, then out of moles.

Mass–Mass Conversions

Grams of one substance to grams of another.

Mass–Mass Conversions

An airbag must fill with exactly the right amount of gas in a fraction of a second, and a pharmaceutical company must know how many grams of each ingredient to buy. Both problems start with a mass of one substance and end with a mass of another. Because substances react in mole ratios, not mass ratios, the route always goes through moles.

  • If we are given the mass of a substance consumed or produced in a reaction, we can determine the corresponding mass of another substance consumed or produced in the same reaction.
    • However, their masses are not necessarily equal.
    • Substances must react with one another in the mole ratio given by the balanced equation.
  • Steps to Performing Mass–Mass Conversions:
    1. Convert the mass of the given substance into moles using its molar mass.
    2. Convert the moles of the given substance into moles of the target substance using the mole ratio.
    3. Convert the moles of the target substance into mass using its molar mass.

Part3

Multistep Mole Conversions

Any unit of one substance to any unit of another.

Multistep Mole Conversions

Real problems mix units: a chemist may know the mass of a solid reactant and need the volume of gas it will produce. Joining two mole triangles with the mole ratio covers every possible combination.

  • Steps to Performing Multistep Mole Conversions:
    1. Convert the given unit into moles.
    2. Convert moles of the given substance into moles of the target substance (if necessary).
    3. Convert moles of the target substance into the desired unit.

Three conversion factors: into moles, mole ratio, out of moles.
