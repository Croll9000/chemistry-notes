Bonding Comparison

Ice melts at 0 °C, table salt at 801 °C and iron at 1538 °C. Each melting point reflects the particles in the solid and the forces that hold them together: weak attractions between water molecules, ionic bonds between Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions, and metallic bonds between iron ions and their sea of electrons. In this section, we will compare ionic, metallic, molecular and network substances by their particles, forces and properties.
