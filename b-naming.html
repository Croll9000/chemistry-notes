Ionic or Covalent? Naming & Formulas

MgCl2 is magnesium chloride, but SCl2 is sulfur dichloride. Before you can name a compound or write its formula, you have to decide which set of rules to use.

Which Set of Rules?

The type of compound decides how it is named.

Deciding: Ionic or Covalent

Chemists name ionic and covalent compounds differently because the two kinds of compounds are built differently. Ionic formulas are fixed by the charges of the ions, while two nonmetals can combine in several ratios.

  • Ask: does the formula or name contain a metal or a polyatomic ion?
    • Yes → ionic compound (no prefixes). Ex: NaCl, Fe2O3, NH4NO3
    • No (two nonmetals) → covalent compound (prefixes). Ex: CO2, N2O4, PCl3
  • A compound that contains a polyatomic ion is ionic, even if every element in it is a nonmetal (ex: NH4Cl).
  • A metalloid bonded to a nonmetal is named like a covalent compound (ex: SiCl4 is silicon tetrachloride).
